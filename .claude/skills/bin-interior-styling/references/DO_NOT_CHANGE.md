# Design Lock — Do Not Change Without Permission

The following items are locked unless the current request explicitly names them.

## Architecture and geometry
- Room envelope, wall positions, dimensions, thicknesses, and circulation.
- Ceiling planes, beams, soffits, coves, curves, radii, and transitions.
- Door/window locations, dimensions, frames, and opening directions.
- Niches, built-ins, cabinetry, shelves, and joinery dimensions.

## Furniture and fixed objects
- Existing furniture identity, size, location, orientation, and silhouette.
- Existing TV, appliances, sanitary fixtures, and fixed equipment.
- Existing decorative elements unless specifically requested to change them.

## Finishes and lighting
- Existing wall, ceiling, floor, cabinetry, and furniture materials.
- Existing color palette and texture scale.
- Lighting fixtures, positions, direction, shadow pattern, and exposure.

## Image framing
- Camera position and angle.
- Perspective and focal impression.
- Composition, framing, and aspect ratio.

## Exceptions
A lock is released only for the specific item explicitly requested. Changing one
item does not release adjacent locks. For example:
- Changing a wall finish does not authorize moving the TV or door.
- Removing an upholstered headboard does not authorize removing the wood wall panel.
- Replacing a bedside cabinet does not authorize changing the bed or room geometry.
- Moving a curtain does not authorize changing the window or ceiling.

Clarify before making a consequential change if the target or boundary is ambiguous.
